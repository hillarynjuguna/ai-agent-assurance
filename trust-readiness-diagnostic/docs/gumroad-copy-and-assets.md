### 3. Short Product Summary

### 4. Full Product Description

**Can Your AI Agent Workflow Survive Outside Scrutiny?**

Building agentic AI workflows is fast. Passing third-party due diligence is not. Before a payment processor issues high-volume merchant rails, an investor signs a term sheet, an insurer underwrites cyber coverage, or an enterprise CISO approves vendor procurement, they will ask hard questions about your agent's autonomy and containment.

The **Agent Trust Readiness Report** provides a clear, 10-dimension pre-diligence evaluation of your agentic AI system. Instead of vague maturity checklists, this report translates your operational controls into direct counterparty readiness signals across five critical trust gates: Payment Processors, Investors, Insurers, Enterprise Clients, and Regulators.

**What This Report Does:**
This report identifies capability and assurance deficits before external auditors flag them. It translates your diagnostic inputs into structured risk zones, counterparty readiness summaries, and prioritized remediation actions.

**What You Receive:**
- Complete 10-dimension pre-diligence diagnostic analysis
- Capability score (0 to 100%) and Assurance score (0 to 100%) breakdown
- Counterparty Trust Gate readiness evaluation across 5 key external profiles
- Complete evidence checklist mapping required artifacts for each dimension
- Priority remediation action plan sorted by risk severity
- Counterparty DDQ defense questions and rationale

**Who It Is For:**
Designed for AI founders, agentic workflow builders, fintech operators, and agency teams preparing client systems for external due diligence.

**Fulfillment & Early-Access Delivery:**
This product is currently in early-access manual fulfillment phase. After completing checkout, you will receive direct email delivery of your custom report package within 12 business hours. If you ran the diagnostic tool first, please include your Report ID at checkout so we can link your diagnostic payload.

**Important Pre-Diligence Disclaimer:**
This diagnostic is a pre-diligence evaluation aid. It does not constitute formal legal, regulatory, cyber insurance, payment processor, or investment advice. Scores rely on user-provided operational details. External counterparty acceptance is subject to independent audit and is not guaranteed.

### 8. FAQ

**Q1: Is this legal or regulatory advice?**
A: No. This report is a pre-diligence diagnostic aid designed to help technical teams identify governance gaps. It is not legal, regulatory, insurance, or financial advice.

**Q2: Is this an official certification?**
A: No. Third-party counterparties (processors, insurers, investors) perform their own independent audits. This report helps you prepare for those audits, but does not grant official certification.

**Q3: How fast do I receive my report?**
A: Reports are currently fulfilled manually during our early-access phase. You will receive your custom report via email within 12 business hours of purchase.

**Q4: What information do I need to provide?**
A: You will need to provide your workflow title, risk classification, primary operational goals, and answers to the 10 diagnostic dimensions (or your Report ID if created via the diagnostic tool).

**Q5: Does this guarantee payment processor or investor approval?**
A: No tool can guarantee approval by third parties. This report highlights key control gaps so you can address them before external scrutiny occurs.

**Q6: What is agent trust readiness?**
A: Agent trust readiness measures whether an autonomous AI system possesses sufficient operational controls (capability) and verifiable proof (assurance) to satisfy external scrutiny.

**Q7: Can I use this for investor due diligence?**
A: Yes. The report provides a structured governance breakdown that demonstrates proactive control over agent autonomy, blast radius, and audit provenance.

**Q8: Can agencies use this for client deliverables?**
A: Yes. Agencies use this report to show clients that custom agentic workflows have been built with proper governance controls and safety boundaries.
