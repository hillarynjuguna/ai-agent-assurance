# Track D: The AI Governability Audit

Track D monetizes the disparity between an AI startup's technical claims and their actual operational governance. As AI scales into regulated environments, counterparties (payment processors, insurers, enterprise clients) require verifiable governance, not "AI theater."

This track positions you as the independent auditor who identifies institutional rejection risks *before* the startup loses a major deal or gets de-platformed.

## Core Deliverable
**The Governability Diagnostic Protocol v3.0**
An interactive, 9-dimension scoring instrument that evaluates:
1. Reversibility Classification
2. Audit Provenance
3. Human Override Capability (The Dead-Man's Switch)
4. Behavioral Verification
5. Delegation Boundaries
6. Counterparty Risk Profile
7. Institutional Legibility
8. Model Provenance & Supply Chain
9. Incident Response

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Guided Self-Assessment ($500 – $1,000)
**The Hook:** "Are you ready for enterprise compliance?"
**Delivery:** You run a 60-minute discovery call with the founding team. You screen-share the Diagnostic HTML tool, scoring their answers live. 
**Output:** The exported PDF report with the automatically generated Priority Gaps. Highly scalable, minimal technical diligence required.

### Tier 2: Full Evidence Audit ($3,000 – $7,500)
**The Hook:** "We find the gap between your pitch deck and your codebase before your payment processor does."
**Delivery:** A 3-day deep dive. You require read-access to their GitHub/architecture diagrams and their marketing claims. You rigorously score the 9 dimensions requiring **artifacts** (logs, specs, tests) rather than verbal answers. 
**Output:** A comprehensive 10-page gap analysis exposing "governance theater" (e.g., the Quicken case study where 8 discrepancies were found) with explicit remediation steps.

### Tier 3: Institutional Legibility Package ($5,000 – $15,000+)
**The Hook:** "We package your operational reality into the exact formats banks, insurers, and enterprise compliance officers require."
**Delivery:** Following the Tier 2 audit, you don't just point out the gaps—you build the translation layer. You generate the counterparty-specific documentation bundles (Risk Registers, Incident Playbooks, Architecture Provenance records).
**Output:** A localized data room that allows the startup to pass institutional diligence on the first attempt.
