# DeepSeek vs Codex: Agentic Commerce Response Audit

Date: August 5, 2026

## Bottom Line

DeepSeek independently converged with Codex and Antigravity on the same core answer:

**The fastest realistic zero-capital path is an agentic commerce readiness / GEO / AI visibility service for Shopify and ecommerce merchants.**

That consensus is now strong across three separate model runs.

DeepSeek's strongest contribution is macro validation. It added credible market-size and adoption signals that make the sales narrative easier:

- Juniper Research forecasts agentic commerce transaction value rising from **$8B in 2026** to **$3.5T in 2031**, with **$1.5T by 2030**.
- Bain estimates the US agentic commerce market could reach **$300B-$500B by 2030**, or **15%-25% of US ecommerce**.
- Adobe completed the **$1.9B Semrush acquisition** in April 2026, explicitly tying it to SEO, GEO, and agentic search optimization.
- Morgan Stanley's retailer analysis warns that agentic transactions must be meaningfully incremental to avoid margin damage, which supports the need for readiness, measurement, and channel strategy.

DeepSeek's weakest area is probability calibration. It repeats the same pattern Antigravity had: correct opportunity, inflated certainty.

## What DeepSeek Added That Should Be Merged

1. **Sharper ICP**

   DeepSeek recommends Shopify merchants with **$1M-$10M annual revenue**. This is a better target band than broad "Shopify merchants" because they are:

   - Big enough to pay $500-$2,000.
   - Small enough to lack internal AI/search teams.
   - Likely already spending on SEO, paid ads, agencies, or conversion optimization.

2. **More concrete first offer**

   DeepSeek suggests:

   - $500 basic audit.
   - $2,000 comprehensive audit.
   - Free 3-SKU mini-audit as lead magnet.

   This is useful. I would adjust to:

   - Free teaser: 3 SKUs and 5 buyer-intent prompts.
   - Paid snapshot: $750.
   - Full audit: $1,500-$2,500.
   - Retainer: $500-$1,500/month.

3. **Adobe/Semrush as sales proof**

   This is a strong outbound hook:

   > Adobe paid $1.9B for Semrush because brand visibility is moving from search engines into AI interfaces and agents. Smaller Shopify merchants need a lighter-weight version of that same visibility layer.

4. **Agent traffic analysis as a second product**

   DeepSeek's #4 idea, "Agent Traffic Analysis," is useful but should come after the readiness audit. It becomes compelling once clients ask: "Are AI systems already visiting us?"

   Add it as an upsell:

   - AI crawler and referral traffic baseline.
   - GA4 / server-log tagging guidance.
   - Bot vs AI assistant hypothesis report.
   - Monthly monitoring.

5. **Avoid enterprise**

   DeepSeek says not to target enterprise first. Correct. Enterprise has money but slow sales, procurement friction, security review, and credibility barriers.

## What DeepSeek Overstated

| DeepSeek claim | Issue | Better framing |

| P($10k/month) = 70% | Much too high. $10k/month requires repeatable sales, delivery quality, and retainers. | 20%-35% in 90-180 days. |
| 5 paid clients in first 30 days | Possible but ambitious without audience or agency partnerships. | Target 2 paid clients in 30 days, stretch goal 5. |
| 50+ clients by Month 3 | Unrealistic for a solo founder doing custom audits. | 5-10 paid projects plus 2-5 retainers is healthier. |
| ChatGPT Checkout Integration as #5 | Premature. ACP/Instant Checkout access and merchant eligibility may be constrained. | Offer "ACP/agent checkout readiness assessment," not full integration promise. |
| AI Product Description Service as #3 | Easy, but commodity and low defensibility. | Bundle into readiness audit as SKU enrichment, not standalone unless sold through marketplaces. |
| "Merchants are desperate" | Too broad. Many merchants are curious, not desperate. | Say "AI visibility is becoming a board-level and marketing-lead concern for merchants with meaningful acquisition spend." |

## How DeepSeek Changes the 30-Day Plan

DeepSeek's best improvement is narrowing the sales target and giving a simple paid ladder.

Updated 30-day plan:

1. Days 1-2: Pick one vertical and build a list of 50 Shopify merchants likely doing $1M-$10M/year.
2. Days 3-4: Run V2 audit snapshots for 10 prospects.
3. Days 5-7: Create 3 polished free mini-audits using real evidence.
4. Days 8-14: Send 50 highly personalized messages, not bulk spam.
5. Days 15-21: Close 1-2 paid audits at $500-$750 founder pricing.
6. Days 22-30: Deliver, collect testimonial, raise price to $1,500, and define retainer.

Stretch goal: $2,000-$5,000 in collected revenue by day 30.

Base goal: one paying client and one credible case study.

## Final Synthesis Across Codex, Antigravity, and DeepSeek

All three runs agree:

- Do not build payment rails.
- Do not build a marketplace first.
- Do not build a consumer shopping agent first.
- Do not raise money.
- Do sell agentic commerce readiness as a service.
- Do target ecommerce merchants before enterprise.
- Do use AI tools to automate audits, reports, and implementation.

The merged best answer is:

**Start with a conservative, evidence-based AI Shopping Visibility Snapshot for Shopify merchants at $1M-$10M revenue. Use the V2 audit tool as the technical spine, add manual AI-answer testing and competitor comparison, then sell implementation and monthly monitoring.**
